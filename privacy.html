Mathsodon privacy policy

Mathsodon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your formulas and settings stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests. When you tap Save, the picture is written to your photo library; when you tap Share, it is handed to the system share sheet and goes only where you send it. The web version at mathsodon.com is a static page served by GitHub Pages, which may log visits in server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Last updated 2026-10-16.
